Who is talking

I'm Laurence. Paramedic, then founder. I still want everything.

I co-founded a coworking at 22, I train for triathlons, I freedive, and I coach women who run their own thing. Multi-passionate is not a phase for me. It is how I am built, and it is exactly why I needed a way to be sharp on one thing without dropping the rest.

Strategy and softness. Both, always. That is the whole show.

The Season Map

There is no right season. There is the one you are in.

Two questions run the whole show. Which season am I in. What does this season need from me. Everything else can be held soft until it is its turn. The same six seasons are the second question of the Reset.

  • Building my career

    Visibility, and one skill that makes you more valuable.

    The trap: saying yes to everything to be seen.

  • Building a business

    One offer, one channel, one number.

    The trap: busywork that feels like building.

  • Reinventing myself

    Self-trust, and a smaller first step.

    The trap: waiting to feel ready.

  • Getting my life together

    One room at a time. Floors, not targets.

    The trap: fixing everything at once.

  • Growing something big

    Patience, consistency, and handing things over.

    The trap: restarting instead of continuing.

  • Slowing down and recalibrating

    Space, and permission to not decide yet.

    The trap: treating rest as a failure of ambition.

What the show believes

You are not behind. You are not broken. You do not need to do everything. Strategy and softness, both, and one sharp thing.
