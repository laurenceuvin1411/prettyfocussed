How it treats you

A safe, warm home for
the business and the life.

Not a second job with a nicer interface. Nothing here keeps score while you are away.

  • no streaks
  • no badges
  • no leaderboards
  • no confetti
  • nothing turns red
  • no guilt email
  • no 4am club
  • no morning routine to earn

One notification a day at most, and it states a fact. Take three weeks off and come back to exactly where you left it, in the same warm room, with nothing counted against you.

No one is watching how early you got up. The system holds the week; you get to put it down.

Founding members

Fifty women are building
the first version with me.

Built alone, this becomes an app that fits exactly one woman's week. Fifty is enough to find where it breaks, and small enough that I answer every message myself.

  • The call

    A small call with me every month while it is being built. Cameras optional, and you can sit there with the mic off.

  • The roadmap

    What this group asks for is what gets built next, in that order. You will watch your own sentence turn into a screen.

  • First access

    Every release lands here first, including the ones that are still wrong. Especially those.

  • A direct line

    My inbox, not a support form. You tell me what broke on a Tuesday and I answer as the person who built it.
