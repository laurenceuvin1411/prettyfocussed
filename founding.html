Questions

What if it opens later than 15 November 2026?
Then you hear it from us before that date, with the new date. Your 14 days only start on the day your account opens.
Can I pay monthly instead?
Not as a founding member. The founding price only exists as a year, because a year is what makes it possible to hold the price for you.
Is this the same as the 30-day plan?
No. The plan is free and stays free. A founding place is the app itself, with your plan already in it on the day you log in.
Do I get an invoice?
Yes. Stripe sends a receipt at once, and an invoice with VAT follows by email. If you have a VAT number, you can enter it on the payment page.
What happens after the first year?
Your account renews at €149 a year. You can stop it in one click, and no one asks you why.
What if the 100 places are gone?
Then this page says so. You can still make your 30-day plan, and you get in when it opens to everyone, at €199 a year.
