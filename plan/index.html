Why Laurence Uvin built Pretty Focussed

Why I built Pretty Focussed

I wanted a free life.

I wanted to build things, travel, look after my health, spend time with the people I love, and still have room to breathe.

For a long time, I thought more options would give me that freedom. So I kept adding more goals, more ideas, more opportunities and more things I could build.

Then I noticed that every new thing had to live somewhere in my week. Each one gave me something else to think about, another decision to make and one more thing competing for my attention.

The thing that gave me the most peace wasn’t something new. It was getting clear on what actually mattered to me and giving it enough space. Some things were allowed to wait, and my life had more room in it because of that.

That’s why I built Pretty Focussed. It shows you where you want your time and energy to go right now, so the things you chose get the space they need.

Laurence Uvin, founder of Pretty Focussed
